## About me:

Hi! My name is Gustavo Ferreira Ribeiro, I'm a QA Engineer with experience in manual testing of corporate web systems, working on the validation of functionalities, business rules, and software quality assurance. I have experience creating, executing, and documenting test cases, running exploratory, functional, regression, and smoke tests, and validating new features during homologation. I've worked on projects in the corporate purchasing, supplier management, CRM (lead generation), and consortium management segments, testing web interfaces. I have experience tracking defects with Jira and SharePoint, organizing test evidence, and collaborating with development teams throughout the entire software development lifecycle.

- Understanding and validating business rules for Grupo Pulsa's purchasing systems, testing shared and specific functionalities across different systems.
- Running exploratory, functional, and smoke tests on Connect, a lead generation and management platform.
- Creating and executing test cases, validating business rules, and homologating features on Remaza Web, a consortium management system.
- Organizing test evidence, documenting scenarios, and tracking defects using Jira, following up on fixes with the development team.
